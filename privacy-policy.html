Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart-Mess handles information when you use the Smart-Mess mobile application and related services (“Smart-Mess”, “we”, “us” or “our”). By using the app, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, Smart-Mess may process:

  • Account information: your name, email address, authentication user ID and account creation information.
  • Optional contact information: a mobile phone number that you choose to add to your member profile.
  • Mess and membership information: mess name and code, membership status, role, join requests and membership-period records.
  • Accounting and activity information: expenses, payer and participant details, deposits, meal counts, tasks, notifications and related dates or notes that members enter while using the app.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact us for help.

Please do not enter passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information into free-text notes.

2. How we use information

Information is used to create and secure accounts, connect members to a mess, display shared records, calculate and present mess accounts, synchronize information across devices, provide app features, respond to support requests, and protect the service against misuse.

3. Shared mess information

Smart-Mess is designed for shared accounting. Information entered into a mess—such as member names and phone numbers, expenses, deposits, meal counts and tasks—may be visible to other active members of that same mess according to the app's access controls. Only add information that is appropriate to share with your mess members.

4. Service providers and storage

Smart-Mess uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support sign-in and store/synchronize app information. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also processes information as part of its sign-in service. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Data may be stored or processed on infrastructure located in countries other than your own.

Google privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Disclosure

We do not publish your private account information as a public directory. Mess information is shared with authorized members of the relevant mess so that shared features can work. Information may also be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Data retention and account requests

We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide Smart-Mess, maintain account and accounting history, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations or protect the service. Historical accounting records may need to be retained even when an account is closed; where appropriate and technically feasible, personal identifiers can be removed or anonymized while preserving necessary accounting records.

To ask about, correct or request deletion of your account information, visit our Account Deletion page or email rubelkhanc2@gmail.com from your registered email address and describe your request. We may ask for information to verify that you control the account. Requests involving an admin account may require transfer of mess administration first. Some records may be retained where necessary for legitimate accounting, security or legal purposes.

Important: Account deletion is not currently offered as an automated in-app feature. Contact us by email to make an account-related request. We will review the request and explain any steps or limitations before processing it.

7. Security

We use Firebase authentication and database access rules intended to restrict access to authorized users. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in credentials private and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Children

Smart-Mess is intended for people who can independently manage a mess account and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your choices and rights

You may update certain profile details through the app. You may also contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable data-protection laws.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Smart-Mess changes. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Contact us

For questions or privacy requests, contact rubelkhanc2@gmail.com.
