Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Smart-Mess account and associated personal information by following the steps below.

How to submit a deletion request

Complete the secure request form using the link below. Enter the email address registered with your Smart-Mess account and the name you use in the app. A mess code and reason are optional.

Submit account deletion request

Never share your password or one-time verification code. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Contact email: rubelkhanc2@gmail.com

What happens after the request?

We will review the request and verify that it comes from the account owner. We will explain the next steps and any records that must be retained. This page submits a request by email; it does not instantly or automatically delete an account.

What happens to mess records?

Smart-Mess supports shared accounting. Expenses, deposits, meal counts and other historical accounting records may need to remain available to preserve the accuracy of the mess accounts. Where appropriate and technically feasible, personal identifiers may be removed or anonymized while necessary accounting records are retained.

If you are the administrator of a mess, you may need to transfer administration to another active member before your account can be deleted. Please arrange this with your mess members first.

Processing and questions

We will review each request individually. Some information may be retained where necessary for accounting, security, dispute resolution or legal obligations. For questions, email rubelkhanc2@gmail.com.
